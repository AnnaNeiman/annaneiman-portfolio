Hi! I'm Anna… Who are you?

Senior Product Designer who designs and builds — 5+ years shipping product UI, component systems, and the front-end that makes them real. I turn messy problems into flows that move the business number, and I'm fluent with the AI tools that speed the work without cutting the judgment. Open to senior product-design and design-engineering roles.
I own interaction and systems end to end: state-driven component architecture, responsive layout across every breakpoint, and the design-system home a team can build on. I pair creative instinct with data-driven rationale, bring accessibility in as a baseline, and make trade-offs explicit so the team can move fast and still ship the right thing.
I come from a dev background, so my designs land as real, accessible front-end — semantic HTML, design tokens, WCAG AA, and clean handoff. This whole site is hand-built, no framework, as the proof. I use Figma, React, Tailwind, and AI tooling (Kiro, MCPs) on the job, and I know where the model helps and where human judgment has to own the call.
